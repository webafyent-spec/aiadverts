An online store is only as convincing as its product photos. There's no physical store to walk into, no product to pick up and examine — the image on the screen has to do all the work of building trust and closing the sale. For South African e-commerce brands, that puts an enormous amount of pressure on product photography that most stores, especially smaller ones, simply can't produce at the volume they need.

A catalogue with twenty, fifty or two hundred SKUs doesn't need one great photo. It needs consistent, professional imagery across every single product — and that's before you get to the video content needed for ads, social media and retargeting campaigns.

## The Catalogue Photography Problem

Producing product imagery at e-commerce scale involves a different set of constraints than a single campaign shoot:

- Every SKU needs at least one clean, consistent product shot for the store listing
- Variants — colours, sizes, finishes — multiply the number of images needed per product
- Lifestyle and context shots are needed on top of clean product shots for marketing and ads
- New stock needs new imagery on a rolling basis, not as a single annual event
- Video content for Meta and TikTok ads is now expected alongside static images, not optional

A photographer charging per product or per hour quickly becomes one of the largest line items in a growing store's budget — and it's a cost that scales directly with catalogue size, which is exactly the direction a growing e-commerce business is moving.

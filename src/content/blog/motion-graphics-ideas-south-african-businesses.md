Most businesses know they should be making video, and most get stuck on the same question: what, exactly, do we put in it? With motion graphics the answer is easier than it looks, because the strongest ideas are simple. A headline, a number, a date, a price, a logo. If you can say it in a few short lines, it can be a motion graphic.

This guide is a companion to our introduction to [motion graphics videos and ads in South Africa](/blog/motion-graphics-videos-ads-south-africa). It's built around ideas you can steal, by industry, with a preview that animates your own business name so you can see how it would feel on a phone.

> **Key takeaways**
>
> - **Motion graphics start from R1,499.** The final price depends on length and complexity.
> - **One idea per video.** A hook, one or two supporting lines and a call to action is enough.
> - **Short lines win.** If it can't be read in a second on a phone, shorten it.
> - **Pick a style that suits your brand.** Slide, pop, fade and typewriter motion all feel different.
> - **Use it alongside AI video adverts.** Footage sells the look, motion graphics carry the details.

## Ideas by Occasion

- **Gifting days:** gift ideas and last-order dates.

## Seven Design Principles for Motion Graphics Adverts

1. **One idea, one hierarchy.** Make the most important line the biggest and let everything else support it.
2. **Contrast over decoration.** Light text on dark or dark on light. If it's hard to read at arm's length, it's hard to read in a feed.
3. **Time it for reading.** Give each line long enough to read once. Our [storyboard builder](/blog/motion-graphics-videos-ads-south-africa) helps you split the seconds.
4. **Let motion guide the eye.** Things that move first get noticed first. Don't animate everything at once.
5. **Stay on brand.** Keep colours, fonts and logo consistent so people recognise you without reading the name.
6. **Respect the safe areas.** Platform buttons cover parts of vertical video. See our [video ad sizes guide](/blog/social-media-video-ad-sizes).
7. **End with one action.** A WhatsApp message, a link or a date.

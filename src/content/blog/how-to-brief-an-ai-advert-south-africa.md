One of the most common questions we get from new clients isn't "does AI advertising work" — by the time a business reaches out, they've usually already seen enough to believe it does. The real question is: "what do you actually need from me?"

It's a fair question, and the answer matters. As a South African agency for AI adverts, the single biggest factor in how good the final advert looks isn't the AI technology itself — it's the quality of the brief and source material we're given to work with. A good brief takes ten minutes to put together and makes the difference between a decent result and an exceptional one.

## What We Actually Need From You

Unlike a traditional shoot, there's no crew, no studio booking, no models to coordinate. What replaces all of that is a clear, well-organised brief. Here's what goes into one:

### 1. Product Photos or References

This is the foundation. Clear, well-lit photos of the actual product — from multiple angles if possible — give the AI production process something accurate to work from. Phone photos are fine as long as they're in focus and show the product clearly; you don't need professional photography to start.

### 2. Brand Assets

Your logo, brand colours, and any existing visual identity guidelines. If your brand already has a look — a colour palette, a font, a mood — we want to match it, not create something that feels disconnected from everything else you've published.

### 3. The Goal of the Advert

Is this for a product launch, a seasonal promotion, brand awareness, or driving traffic to a specific offer? The goal shapes the tone, pacing and call to action of the final piece. An advert built for a flash sale looks and feels different from one built for long-term brand building.

### 4. Where It's Going to Run

A video for Instagram Reels needs different pacing and framing than one for a website hero banner or a WhatsApp Status update. Telling us the platform upfront means the advert is built correctly the first time, rather than reformatted after the fact.

### 5. Examples You Like

If you've seen an advert — yours, a competitor's, or something completely unrelated — that captures a mood or style you want, share it. It's one of the fastest ways to close the gap between what's in your head and what we produce.

### 6. Any Non-Negotiables

Specific claims that must appear, compliance requirements, things that absolutely cannot be shown or implied. Better to flag these upfront than catch them in revisions.

## What You Don't Need to Worry About

You don't need professional copywriting, a shot list, a storyboard, or technical specifications. You don't need to know what "cinematic" or "macro" mean in production terms — that's our job to translate from your brief into the finished advert. The goal of the brief is to get us the raw information; how it becomes a finished piece of content is on us.

Property sells on first impression, and in 2026 that first impression happens on a phone screen before a buyer ever books a viewing. A listing with flat, poorly lit photos and no video gets scrolled past. A listing with a cinematic walkthrough, a drone-style exterior shot and a polished video ad gets saved, shared and enquired about. The gap between the two isn't the property — it's the marketing.

For agents and developers, producing that level of content for every listing has always been a cost and scheduling problem. A videographer, a drone operator, editing time — multiplied across every property on the books — adds up fast, especially for listings that sell within weeks and need the content turned around immediately.

## What Traditional Property Video Production Involves

A proper listing video — the kind that gets shared and drives real enquiries — typically requires:

- A videographer for interior walkthroughs, often across multiple rooms and angles
- A licensed drone operator for aerial establishing shots of the property and surrounding area
- Lighting and staging considerations to make interiors look their best on camera
- Editing time to cut raw footage into a polished, shareable video
- Turnaround that fits the listing timeline — which is rarely the timeline a production schedule allows for

Booking that for a single listing can run R5,000–R15,000 depending on property size and location. For an agent managing multiple listings at once, or a developer marketing an entire complex, that cost and turnaround time becomes the bottleneck that slows down how fast listings get real market exposure.

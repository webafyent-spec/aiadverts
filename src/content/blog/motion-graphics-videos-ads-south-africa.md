## What Are Motion Graphics?

Motion graphics are graphic design that moves. A logo that builds itself on screen, a headline that slides in on the beat, a chart that fills up, an icon that turns into the next icon: that's motion graphics. They're different from live-action film because nothing needs to be photographed, and different from an AI video advert because the look is designed rather than generated as cinematic footage.

That makes them well suited to messages that are about **information** rather than **atmosphere**.

- **Explainer videos.** "How it works in three steps" is the classic use. Service businesses, tech and professional practices use it to turn a complicated offer into something clear. - **Number and result animations.** Counters, percentages and simple charts.
- **Logo and brand animations.** A short animated logo for the start or end of every video.
- **Social adverts with kinetic text.** Many people scroll with the sound off, so adverts built around readable on-screen text still get the message across.
- **Event and launch announcements.** Date, venue and price in a single clear video.
- **WhatsApp Status and Stories.** Short, vertical, one idea. See our [WhatsApp Status guide](/blog/whatsapp-status-video-ads-south-africa).

## Tips for Better Motion Graphics

1. **One idea per video.** If you need three messages, make three videos.
2. **Win the first three seconds.** Open with the strongest line, number or visual, not a logo.
3. **Make it work without sound.** Many people watch on mute, so keep the key words on screen.
4. **Keep text short and large.** If a line is hard to read on a phone in a second, shorten it. Our [video ad sizes guide](/blog/social-media-video-ad-sizes) covers safe zones.
5. **Stay on brand.** Use the same colours, fonts and logo in every video, so your adverts build recognition.
6. **End with one clear action.** A WhatsApp number, a link or a date.
7. **Test two hooks.** See our guide to [A/B testing adverts on a small budget](/blog/ab-test-adverts-small-budget-south-africa).
